About NewsBoard

NewsBoard is a newsroom tool for quickly checking the lead story or centerpiece across major news sites. It tracks homepage changes over time, making it easy to see what moved, when headlines or links shifted, and to review editorial decisions around breaking news moments. AP via Google News is a separate, unranked story-discovery feed of AP articles indexed by Google, not AP homepage tracking. Its highlight is the latest published discovery; links open through Google News. Archived AP homepage observations keep their original Associated Press label.
